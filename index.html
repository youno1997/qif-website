Questions

What exactly is included? +
Everything: prayer times and athan, the complete Quran word by word with audio, Quran Radio with offline downloads, a digital tasbih, six dhikr games with a leaderboard, The Scale, illustrated wudu and prayer guides, a Qibla compass, the Hijri calendar and streaks — plus the prayer-time pause and daily screen-time limits.
Do I still need my other Islamic apps? +
That’s the idea — you shouldn’t. Qif covers prayer times, Quran, dhikr, Qibla and tracking in one place, and adds what no separate app can: it pauses the distractions when it’s time to pray.
Is Qif free? +
Qif has a Supporter plan. If you can’t afford it right now, choose “I can’t afford it right now” and you get full access for free.
How do I unlock my apps? +
Remember Allah. Say a dhikr out loud — Qif listens and counts — or read a few verses of the Quran. Then your apps open again.
Can Qif limit my screen time too? +
Yes. Set a daily limit for any app. When you reach it, Qif pauses it until you say dhikr or read Quran — so scrolling time turns into worship. Wellness and one-tap emergency pauses are built in.
What is The Scale? +
A simple picture of your day. The verses you read, the dhikr you say and the prayers you pray go on one side; time on your phone goes on the other.
Does Qif show ads or sell my data? +
No. There are no ads in Qif, and your data is never sold.
Can I keep my progress on a new phone? +
Yes. Make an account and your progress syncs, so it follows you to a new iPhone.
What languages does Qif support? +
Twelve: English, Spanish, Arabic, Turkish, Malay, Indonesian, Urdu, Bengali, Hindi, French, Farsi and Hausa — with full right-to-left layout for Arabic, Urdu and Farsi.
